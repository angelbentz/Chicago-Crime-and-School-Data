          School DataCrime Data

      This page presents interactive visualizations that explore patterns and potential correlations between crime rates and educational outcomes across Chicago's community areas. By examining data such as graduation rates alongside crime statistics, this dashboard aims to highlight trends that may inform community development, policy decisions, and public awareness. Users can investigate how educational achievement and public safety intersect geographically, providing insight into the broader social landscape of the city.

      Crime Intensity Map

      This map displays crime intensity and graduation rates by location. The size of the circles correspond to areas with greater rates of crime and the color scale correlates to the graduation rate. Based on this map it is clear that a majority of crime is centered in the center of Chicago with the southern and northern areas having much smaller concentrations. There are also several outliers in this data with several of the locations with the highest graduation rates also having fairly high rates of crime. This data could possibly be skewed by other factors such as population. At the same time several of the communities with high rates of crime also have relatively low rates of graduation which aligns with the precedence that was set by the choropleth maps of crime and education activity for local communities. While this data is partially inconsistent with expectations, it does align in some areas with the expected behavior.
